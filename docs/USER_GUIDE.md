# SignLand - Complete Usage Guide

## 🎯 How to Use SignLand

SignLand has **TWO MODES** for communication:

---

## 📝 MODE 1: ASL Alphabet Mode (Letter-by-Letter Spelling)

### What You're Currently Using ✅

This mode lets you **spell words letter by letter** using ASL alphabet signs.

### How It Works:

1. **Sign letters one at a time** (A, B, C, D, etc.)
2. **Letters appear in "CURRENT WORD"** box (e.g., "H-E-L-L-O")
3. **Speak the word** when done:
   - Hold **👍 Thumbs Up** for 1 second, OR
   - Click **"Speak Word"** button
4. **Word is spoken** and added to Word History
5. **Start next word** - repeat the process

### Example: Spelling "HELLO"

```
Step 1: Sign "H" → Shows: H|
Step 2: Sign "E" → Shows: HE|
Step 3: Sign "L" → Shows: HEL|
Step 4: Sign "L" → Shows: HELL|
Step 5: Sign "O" → Shows: HELLO|
Step 6: Hold 👍 Thumbs Up 1s → Speaks "HELLO"
```

### Control Gestures in Alphabet Mode:

| Gesture | Hold Time | Action |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| 👍 Thumbs Up | 1 second | Speak current word |
| ✋ Flat Hand (palm forward) | 1 second | Speak current word |
| ✊ Closed Fist | 2 seconds | Speak entire sentence |
| 👍 Shake Thumb | Quick shake | Delete last letter (backspace) |

### Word Predictions 🎯

After typing 3+ letters, you'll see **word suggestions**:
- Top 3 predictions appear
- Click any prediction to auto-complete
- Saves time on common words

## 🎓 Complete Workflow Example

### Scenario: "I AM HUNGRY"

**Method 1: Spell Each Word**

1. **Spell "I"**:
   - Sign letter "I"
   - Hold 👍 Thumbs Up 1s
   - Hears: "I"

2. **Spell "AM"**:
   - Sign letter "A"
   - Sign letter "M"
   - Hold 👍 Thumbs Up 1s
   - Hears: "AM"

3. **Spell "HUNGRY"**:
   - Sign: H-U-N-G-R-Y
   - Hold 👍 Thumbs Up 1s
   - Hears: "HUNGRY"

4. **Speak Full Sentence**:
   - Hold ✊ Closed Fist 2s
   - Hears: "I am hungry" (with natural grammar)

**Word History Shows**:
```
#1 I
#2 AM
#3 HUNGRY
```

---

## 🎯 Why You're Seeing "O", "X", "U" Separately

You're in **Alphabet Mode**, which detects individual letters, not full words.

### To Spell a Complete Word:

1. **Sign all letters** of the word (e.g., H-E-L-L-O)
2. **Wait for letters to appear** in "CURRENT WORD" box
3. **Commit the word** with 👍 Thumbs Up (1s hold)
4. **Word is spoken** as a complete word

### Common Mistake:

❌ **Don't commit after each letter** - this creates single-letter "words"
✅ **Commit after spelling the full word**

## 📜 Word History Scrolling

### Manual Scrolling (Your Request) ✅

- **Purple scrollbar** on right side (12px thick)
- **Scroll manually** up/down to see all words
- **No auto-scroll** - you control when to scroll
- **"↑ Scroll to top"** button to jump to first word

### How to Use:

1. **New words appear** at bottom of list
2. **Scroll down manually** to see latest words
3. **Scroll up** to see older words
4. **Click "↑ Scroll to top"** to jump to #1

1. **Click "Start Camera"** button

2. **Sign letter "I"** → See "HI|" in Current Word

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**Q: Why do I see single letters (O, X, U) as separate words?**
A: You're committing after each letter. Wait until you spell the full word before holding 👍 Thumbs Up.

**Q: How do I see older words?**
A: Scroll up using the purple scrollbar on the right side.

**Q: Can I use full sign language phrases?**
A: Currently, only 7 gesture phrases are available (Yes, No, Hello, etc.). For complex sentences, use Alphabet Mode to spell words.

**Q: How do I delete a letter?**
A: Shake your 👍 Thumb left-right quickly (backspace gesture).
